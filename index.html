How to Play

- Click on the character you like better from the two or tie them if you like them equally or don't know them.
- Don't forget to click [Filter by Series Entry]! By doing so, you can select the characters based on their verses or mashes it up into any category you like!
- You can click the light/dark version toggle on right top side for your preference!



Keyboard controls

H/LeftArrow (pick left) | J/DownArrow (undo) | K/UpArrow (tie) | L/RightArrow (pick right) | S (save progress).

Before sorting: S/Enter (start sorting) | L (load progress).

1/2/3 always correspond to the first/second/third buttons.
